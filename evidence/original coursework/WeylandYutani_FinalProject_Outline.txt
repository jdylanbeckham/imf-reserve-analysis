Slide 2::: Project Purpose
Weyland-Yutani is trying to understand world domination more clearly.  
They have asked for an analysis of the world's international reserves to identify countries with weak financial buffers to support investment and policy development as the ideal "Global Company."

Slide 3::: Understanding the IMF Dataset
Source: International Monetary Fund (IMF)  
Scope: Global financial reserves by country and indicator  
Years Covered: 2020–2023  
Format: Wide format, annual frequency  
Key Fields: Country, Indicator, Year, Value

Slide 4::: Preparing Data for Analysis
• Uploaded IMF CSV to Google Cloud Storage  
• Imported into BigQuery dataset `global_reserves`  
• Reshaped data using `UNPIVOT` into year-value pairs  
• Filtered out nulls for clean queries

Slide 5::: Exploratory SQL Analysis
• Validated years 2020–2023 are populated in dataset  
• Identified key indicators: gold reserves, foreign asset claims  
• Confirmed tidy (year-value) structure using SQL and `UNPIVOT`
